Multimodal Asset Recovery AI

AI-powered visual and multilingual retrieval for lost & found and urban asset recovery.

CLIP ViT-B/32 | SigLIP2 So400M

Al-Amen Technology's Urban Intelligence platform runs two AI engines side by side on this festival canary -- CLIP ViT-B/32 (production baseline) and SigLIP2 So400M (experimental) -- converting visual and textual evidence into comparable, model-specific representations, retrieving relevant candidates, and combining them with OCR and barcode signals. Final match decisions remain governed by the Urban Intelligence Backend and human review.
